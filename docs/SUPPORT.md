# JobLynk Support Workflow

## Candidate support inbox

**Email:** support@joblynk.co.za  
**Managed by:** Shabnam's team

## Routing

| Query type | Assign to | Response SLA |
|------------|-----------|--------------|
| Candidate account, profile, applications | Shabnam's team via support@joblynk.co.za | 1 business day |
| POPIA / data deletion (candidates) | Information Officer (info@joblynk.co.za) with support CC | 2 business days |
| Recruiter billing, job posts, packages | info@joblynk.co.za | 1 business day |
| Technical outages | Engineering escalation from support@joblynk.co.za | Same day |

## Setup checklist (Afrihost / cPanel)

1. Create mailbox or forwarder: `support@joblynk.co.za` → Shabnam's team distribution list.
2. Add auto-reply: confirm receipt and expected response time.
3. Route `info@joblynk.co.za` for general and POPIA Information Officer queries.
4. Test from an external address after DNS propagates.
