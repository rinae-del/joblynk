FAQ

Everything you wanted to ask.

Quick answers to common questions. Need more? Email our team.

Is JobLynk really free for job seekers?

Yes — building your CV, generating cover letters, browsing live jobs and applying are 100% free. No credit card, no trial period, no upsells.

Are the jobs on JobLynk verified?

Every recruiter and company is verified before they can post. We don't scrape listings from third-party sites — every job comes directly from a real SA employer.

How does the AI cover letter work?

Paste any job description and our AI tailors a personalised cover letter using your profile, skills and experience — ready to download or copy in seconds.

Will my CV pass ATS systems?

Our templates are designed and tested specifically for Applicant Tracking Systems — clean structure, parseable text, and the right keyword placement.

How do recruiters get started?

Create a recruiter account, verify your company details, then choose the package that fits your hiring needs on the dedicated recruiter packages page.

Is my personal data safe?

We're POPIA-compliant, SSL-encrypted, and never sell your data. You control who sees your CV and can delete your account at any time.
