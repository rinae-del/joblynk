FAQ

Everything you wanted to ask.

Quick answers to common questions. Need more? Email our team.

Is JobLynk really free for job seekers?

Yes. Building your CV, generating cover letters, browsing live jobs and applying are 100% free for job seekers.

Are the jobs on JobLynk verified?

Yes. Every recruiter and company is verified before they can post on JobLynk. We review each listing for quality and relevance so you only see real opportunities from legitimate South African recruiters and hiring teams.

Can I apply before creating an account?

Yes. Browse jobs and start an application on the job page. You can create a free account when you submit — your details are saved as you go.

How does the cover letter builder work?

Paste a job description and JobLynk drafts a cover letter using your profile and experience. Review and edit before you download or attach it to an application.

Will my CV pass ATS systems?

Our templates are designed and tested specifically for Applicant Tracking Systems with clean structure, parseable text and the right keyword placement.

How do recruiters get started?

Create a recruiter account, verify your company details, then choose the package that fits your hiring needs on the dedicated recruiter packages page.

Is my personal data safe?

We are POPIA-compliant, SSL-encrypted, and never sell your data. You control who sees your CV and can delete your account at any time.
